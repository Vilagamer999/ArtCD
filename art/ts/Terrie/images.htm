Thumbnails of Terrie's Prints!

Yes, you asked for it, you've got it! Thumbnails of all available prints. Of course, the images are nowhere near as good as the originals - but you get the idea. The images that are from coloured pencil originals (especially the ones on coloured paper) are darker and much less distinct than the original - it's hard to make a thumbnail of them clear - but again, you get the idea. Of course, the items that might have any naughty bits showing do not - not after digital enhancement - but they do in the regular prints. If you're interested in any print, just let me know!

If the name is in red, then the print was new at Mephit FurMeet 2003.
If the name is in blue, then the print was new at ConiFur NorthWest 2003.
If the name is in green, then the print is new before Further Confusion 2004.

If you are getting just this message and no pictures, be patient.
With around 100 pictures, it takes a bit for them to load.
